The process

From intake to
live registry — seven steps

01

Submit intake application

Founders complete a structured intake form covering company stage, sector, and primary capital objective. Thiqa's intake team confirms eligibility and assigns a lead analyst within 5 business days.

02

Upload evidence across seven pillars

A secure, permissioned data room guides founders through document submission for each pillar — governance, financials, market, team, legal, technology, and impact. Automated completeness checks flag gaps before analyst review begins.

03

Analyst audit and verification

A Thiqa-certified analyst independently verifies every submitted document against the published methodology. Where third-party confirmation is required — audited accounts, IP filings, regulatory licences — Thiqa contacts the issuing authority directly.

04

Receive pillar-level scored report

Each of the seven pillars receives a numeric score and qualitative annotation. The aggregate produces the published grade (AA+ to C) alongside a private improvement roadmap visible only to the founder.

05

Publish to the Thiqa registry

The verified grade is version-stamped and published to the Thiqa public registry. Investors, FCPR fund managers, and COSOB portals can query the grade in real time against a cryptographic hash of the underlying evidence set.

06

Share your verified investor dossier

Founders generate a permissioned dossier link — time-limited and auditor-signed — for distribution to prospective investors. Each access event is logged, giving founders precise visibility into investor engagement.

07

Maintain the grade through active surveillance

Thiqa monitors material trigger events — new financials, regulatory changes, leadership changes — and resurveils the grade on a 90-day cycle. Investors always hold a timestamp-verified current signal, not a document dated 18 months ago.
